# AuraCall Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 9, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AuraCall ("AuraCall", the "Bot", "we", "us", or
"our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you invite or
use AuraCall in Discord servers.

AuraCall is designed to process the minimum information needed to provide music,
playlist, voice, command, and service-management features. This policy should be
read together with the [AuraCall Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md), Discord's
own policies, and the policies of any third-party services used with AuraCall.

This policy is a general template and is not legal advice. The project owner should
replace the contact placeholder and confirm the legal basis, retention periods, and
regional requirements before operating a production service.

## 1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to information processed through AuraCall's Discord commands,
voice features, music and playlist features, hosted infrastructure, databases,
documentation, and related service operations.

It does not apply to Discord's own processing, third-party audio providers, hosting
providers, or external links. Those parties publish and control their own privacy
practices.

## 2. Information we may process

Depending on the features used and the configuration of a server, AuraCall may
process the following categories of information:

### Discord account and server information

- Discord user ID;
- Discord username, display name, avatar reference, and related public profile data;
- Discord server ID and server name;
- channel ID, channel name, and voice-channel information;
- role, permission, and membership information needed to authorize commands; and
- interaction, command, and message context needed to respond to requests.

### Music and playlist information

- search terms and requested URLs;
- track titles, artists, durations, identifiers, artwork references, and source URLs;
- queue and playback state;
- playlist names, descriptions, track references, owners, likes, and timestamps; and
- settings such as preferred volume or loop mode where those features are enabled.

### Optional Spotify connection information

If you choose Spotify import, AuraCall receives the Spotify account identifier,
playlist names and track metadata needed to import the selected playlist. OAuth
access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted and are used only to access your
Spotify playlists for this feature. You can revoke AuraCall's access from your
Spotify account settings; operators should also provide a disconnect or deletion
process for stored connection data.

### Technical and operational information

- command execution timestamps and outcomes;
- error, diagnostic, and service-health information;
- hosting, connection, and security logs;
- network or request metadata made available by infrastructure providers; and
- configuration values required to connect services, excluding secrets from routine
  user-facing records.

### Information we do not intentionally request

AuraCall does not intentionally request Discord passwords, payment card details,
government identification, or private message histories. Do not send tokens,
passwords, API keys, or other secrets to AuraCall, its commands, or its maintainers.

## 3. How information is collected

Information may be received when:

1. a server administrator invites or configures AuraCall;
2. a user invokes a command, button, form, or application interaction;
3. a user joins or uses a voice feature involving AuraCall;
4. a user creates, edits, views, or interacts with a playlist;
5. Discord sends the Bot an event required to perform a requested feature; or
6. hosting, database, audio, security, or monitoring systems create operational logs.

## 4. Why we use information

We may use information to:

- authenticate users and check server or channel permissions;
- execute commands and provide requested music or voice functionality;
- search for, retrieve, queue, and play requested tracks;
- save and display playlists, preferences, and queue-related state;
- show bot status, uptime, latency, version, and diagnostics;
- prevent abuse, spam, unauthorized access, and service disruption;
- troubleshoot errors, improve reliability, and maintain infrastructure;
- investigate security incidents and enforce our Terms of Service;
- comply with legal obligations or valid legal requests; and
- communicate important service, security, or policy changes.

We do not use AuraCall data to sell personal profiles or serve behavioral advertising.
If that changes, this policy will be updated before the new practice begins where
required by law.

## 5. Legal bases

Where privacy law requires a legal basis, the project owner may rely on one or more
of the following, depending on the processing:

| Purpose | Potential legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Providing requested commands and features | Performance of a service or legitimate interests |
| Maintaining security and preventing abuse | Legitimate interests or legal obligation |
| Maintaining playlists and preferences | Performance of a service or user request |
| Service analytics and reliability | Legitimate interests, with appropriate safeguards |
| Responding to legal requests | Legal obligation |
| Optional communications | Consent or legitimate interests, as applicable |

The appropriate legal basis depends on the operator, location, service design, and
applicable law.

## 6. Storage and retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes
described in this policy, unless a longer period is required for legal, security,
backup, or dispute-resolution reasons.

| Data category | Typical retention approach |
| --- | --- |
| Playlists and preferences | While the server or feature remains active, unless deleted sooner |
| Queue and playback state | Usually temporary and removed when no longer needed for playback |
| Command and diagnostic logs | For a limited operational period, then deleted or aggregated |
| Security and abuse records | As needed to investigate, prevent, or document incidents |
| Backups | Removed according to the backup cycle, subject to restoration windows |

Actual periods may vary by deployment, database, hosting provider, and legal need.
Server administrators should not assume that deleting a Discord message immediately
deletes related operational records or backups.

## 7. Sharing and disclosures

We may disclose information to:

- Discord, when required for the Bot to receive events and respond to interactions;
- audio, hosting, database, storage, monitoring, and security providers needed to
  operate requested features;
- maintainers, moderators, or administrators who need information to operate or
  secure a server or investigate abuse;
- legal, regulatory, or law-enforcement authorities where legally required or where
  necessary to protect rights, safety, or infrastructure; and
- a successor or service provider involved in a merger, transfer, restructuring,
  or other change in project operation.

We do not sell personal information. We do not disclose information to unrelated
third parties for their own direct marketing.

## 8. Third-party audio and external links

When you search for or play music, query terms, URLs, track identifiers, or related
requests may be sent to the configured audio provider. That provider may collect
additional information under its own policy.

Track URLs, artwork, and external links may lead to services outside AuraCall's
control. Review those services before using them. AuraCall does not guarantee that
external content is accurate, safe, available, or suitable.

## 9. Cookies and similar technologies

The Discord bot itself does not use browser cookies to provide command features.
Project websites, dashboards, documentation hosts, GitHub, or third-party services
may use cookies, local storage, analytics, or similar technologies under their own
policies.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the service,
which may include permission checks, access controls, secret management, logging,
dependency updates, backups, and restricted database access.

No system is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your Discord
account, server permissions, hosting credentials, tokens, and passwords. If you
believe a secret has been exposed, revoke or rotate it immediately and notify the
maintainers without including the secret in your message.

## 11. Children and age-related use

AuraCall is not directed specifically to children. You must comply with Discord's
age requirements and applicable child-privacy laws. Server administrators and
guardians are responsible for deciding whether AuraCall is appropriate for a
particular community.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that violates
applicable law, contact the project owner so the matter can be reviewed.

## 12. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to:

- request access to personal information;
- request correction of inaccurate information;
- request deletion or restriction of processing;
- object to certain processing;
- request portability of information you provided;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
- complain to a relevant data-protection authority.

Some rights are limited by law and may not apply to every data category. We may
need to verify your identity and relationship to the relevant Discord account or
server before handling a request.

To request access, correction, deletion, or clarification, contact the project owner
using the information below. A server administrator may also be able to delete
playlists or remove AuraCall from a server directly.

## 13. International transfers

AuraCall and its providers may process information in countries different from the
country where you live. Where required, the project owner should use appropriate
safeguards for international transfers and explain those safeguards upon request.

## 14. Server administrator responsibilities

Server administrators should:

1. inform members that AuraCall is installed and what features it provides;
2. configure permissions and channels appropriately;
3. avoid placing sensitive information in playlist names, descriptions, or commands;
4. respond to member requests when they control server-specific data; and
5. remove AuraCall if the server no longer accepts its processing practices.

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, providers, data practices, or
legal requirements change. The updated version will show a new effective or last
updated date. For material changes, we may provide additional notice where practical.
